what it's about

efficiency you can build yourself

the idea is simple: through a technical lab with small, sustainability-minded experiments, we show that anyone can improve how things work. A plant that waters itself, a web app that reads the surf without being an engineer.

We start from the finished lab (top-down), break it into two live experiments, then you rebuild them in groups as far as you can get. You leave with a real web app to keep playing at home.
